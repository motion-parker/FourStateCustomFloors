Should I pick LVP or hardwood?

LVP handles water, pets and kids better and usually costs less. Hardwood is real wood and can be refinished. We'll help you decide what fits each room.

Is LVP waterproof?

Most LVP is water-resistant on the surface, but water can still get under it at the edges. That's why the install and transitions matter in kitchens and baths.
