Questions, answered.

Should I pick LVP or hardwood?

LVP handles water, pets and kids better and usually costs less. Hardwood is real wood and can be refinished. Tim can walk you through which fits each room.

Is LVP waterproof?

Most LVP is water-resistant to waterproof on the surface. Water can still get under it at the edges, so the install and transitions matter in kitchens and baths.
