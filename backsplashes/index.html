Questions, answered.

Have a question that isn't here? Call 417-825-8240 and talk it over.

How much does a backsplash cost?

Most kitchen backsplashes we install run $800 to $2,000. The size of the wall, the tile and the number of outlets and windows change the price.

How long does a backsplash take?

Most take [X to X] days. Bigger or patterned jobs can take longer.

Do I need to clear off my counters?

Yes, clear the counters along the wall being tiled. We cover them and your cabinets before we start.

What happens with the outlets?

We cut around them so the covers sit flush with the new tile. [Confirm with Tim: whether we add outlet extenders.]

Can you remove my old backsplash?

Yes. The old tile comes out with dust control and the wall gets patched before the new tile goes up.

Do I have to pick out and buy the tile?

You pick out and buy the tile, so you get exactly the look you want. Our price covers the setting and Schluter waterproofing materials, and we're happy to help you choose.
