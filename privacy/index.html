Privacy Policy.

Last updated November 16, 2026

Four State Custom Floors ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This page explains, in plain terms, what information we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you fill out our estimate form, call or text us, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number and town
  • Details about your project and any photos you send
  • Basic website data, like which page you came from, to help us understand how people find us

How we use it

  • To contact you about your project and give you an estimate
  • To schedule and do the work
  • To send project updates, and after the job, a request for a review

Text messages

If you give us your phone number, you agree that we can text you about your project. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop texts, or HELP for help.

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or text message consent with anyone for their marketing. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We only share it with the tools that help us run the business (like our scheduling, texting and email software), and only so they can do that job for us. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No system is perfect, but we only keep what we need to serve you.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information, or stop contacting you, at any time. Just call or text 417-825-8240.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new date.
