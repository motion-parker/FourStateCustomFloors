What a bad tile job costs you.

Most of it you cannot see until it is too late.

  • Leaks behind the wall.

    Skip the waterproofing and water soaks the studs and subfloor for years before you notice.

  • Mold you cannot reach.

    Wet framing grows mold. Fixing it means tearing everything out.

  • Cracked grout and loose tile.

    A bad base or the wrong thinset shows up as cracks within a year or two.

  • Paying for it twice.

    A cheap shower that fails in 3 years costs more than a good one built once.
