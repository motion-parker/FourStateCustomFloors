What a bad tile job costs you.

Most of it hides behind the tile, where you won't see it for years.

  • Leaks behind the wall.

    Skip the waterproofing and water soaks the studs and subfloor for years before you notice.

  • Mold you can't reach.

    Wet framing grows mold. Fixing it means tearing everything out.

  • Cracked grout and loose tile.

    A bad base under the tile shows up as cracks within a year or two.

  • Paying for it twice.

    A cheap shower that fails in a few years costs more than one built right the first time.
