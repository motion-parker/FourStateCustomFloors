Questions, answered.

Have a question that isn't here? Call 417-825-8240 and talk it over.

Is it really dust-free?

No removal is completely dust-free, which is why we call it dust-controlled. With the room sealed and HEPA vacuums and an air scrubber running, the dust stays in the work area instead of spreading through your house.

Can I stay home during removal?

Yes. The work area is sealed off, so most homeowners stay home. It is loud while the tile is coming up.

How long does tile removal take?

Most rooms take 1 to 2 days, depending on the floor and what's under it. Whole-house removal and thick mortar beds take longer.

Do you haul the old tile away?

Yes. Debris is hauled off and the work area is cleaned before we leave.

Can you remove tile without installing new flooring?

Yes. We'll take out the old tile even if you have someone else installing the new floor, or you're doing it yourself.

What does removal cost?

Most removals run about $4 per square foot. Thick mortar beds and glued-down tile can cost more. You get a written quote first.
